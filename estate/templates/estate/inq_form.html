Common Questions

Quick answers before you send

Will my contact details be made public?

No. Your name, phone number, and email are only shared with the specific agent or company who listed the property. Estate Web does not publish your details or share them with other users.

How quickly will the agent reply?

Most agents respond within 24 hours. If you haven't heard back in 2 days, try calling the phone number listed on the property detail page directly.

Do I need to pay anything to send an inquiry?

No. Sending an inquiry is completely free. Estate Web does not charge buyers or renters for using the platform. Never pay anyone claiming to collect a fee on behalf of Estate Web.

What happens after I send the inquiry?

Your message is saved as a lead in the agent's dashboard. The agent will see your name, phone number, and message, then contact you through your chosen method — WhatsApp, phone call, or email.

Can I schedule a viewing through this form?

Yes — use the optional "Preferred Viewing Date" field to suggest a date. If you skip it, the agent will work out a suitable time with you directly after you connect.

Is the property still available?

Listings marked as "Available" on Estate Web are expected to be active. Agents are required to remove or update sold/rented properties within 48 hours. If you suspect a listing is stale, send the inquiry and confirm availability with the agent directly.

Does Estate Web handle the transaction?

No. Estate Web is a listing platform only. All payments, agreements, and viewings happen directly between you and the agent or property owner. To avoid scams, always inspect the property in person or send a trusted agent to verify it before making any payments.
