🛡️ Estate Web Safety Tips

Protect yourself from common scams in Nigeria's real estate market

🚫

No Inspection Fees Upfront

Never pay 'inspection fees' before seeing the property. Legitimate agents show it for free. If they insist, walk away—it's a scam.

🕵️

Spot the 'Deep Pocket' Trick

Agent shows a suspiciously cheap listing to lure you, then 'pivots' to a more expensive one? Deep Pocket scam. Leave immediately.

⚖️

Verify Legal Fees Go to a Real Lawyer

Demand the lawyer's name, Bar ID, and contact. Call to confirm before paying any 'legal fees'. Agents printing fake docs is common.

💳

Rent to Landlord/Manager Only

Pay to the landlord's verified account or corporate manager, never a middleman's personal bank (Opay, Palmpay, etc.).

📢

Report Overpriced/Illegal Fees

Spot non-compliant fees? Flag the listing on Estate Web. We advocate for you and take action against bad actors.

📄

Demand Title Documents

Ask for C of O, Deed, or Survey Plan copies upfront. Verify at Lands Registry if possible.

✅

Only Deal with Verified Profiles

Check the green badge. Unverified agents? Proceed with extra caution.

👥

Bring a Trusted Friend/Agent

Never view alone. Have a second pair of eyes for measurements, photos, and negotiations.
