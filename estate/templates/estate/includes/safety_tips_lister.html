Protect Your Property

Simple steps to avoid bad tenants and squatter disputes.

Verify Work & Bank Before Signing

Request 3 months bank statements and an employer letter. No verifiable income means no lease.

Clear All Utility Bills First

Get receipts for NEPA/PHCN, water, and waste levy before handing over keys. Don't inherit debt.

Collect 1-Year Rent + 2 Months Security

Never accept less upfront. Security deposit covers damages discovered after they move out.

Early-Exit Penalty in the Agreement

If a tenant needs to break the lease, charge at least 3 months rent penalty. Put it in the contract.

Protect Your Commission

Stop tenants cutting you out after viewings.

Never Give Out the Landlord's Number

Direct all contact through Estate Web. If a tenant calls the landlord directly, you lose your commission.

Run Group Viewings, Not Solo Ones

Always bring a colleague. Having a witness protects you from false claims and commission disputes.

Sign Your Commission Agreement Before Viewings

Get 10% commission in a signed agreement first. A verbal promise is not enforceable.

Use Estate Web for All Communication

Every message and call logged on the platform is your proof if a dispute arises.

Scale Without Risk

Keep your portfolio and agents well-managed.

Verify All Sub-Agents Before Onboarding

Check government-issued ID and Estate Web profiles before letting any agent handle your listings.

Track Every Viewing via the Dashboard

Estate Web logs all activity. Use your analytics to spot unusual commission patterns early.

Use Standard Lawyer-Reviewed Contracts

No verbal agreements between agents and clients. Every deal needs a signed document.

Keep Company Listings Under Central Control

Only verified company agents should have access to list or delist properties on your behalf.

Estate Web logs every viewing, message, and inquiry. Your activity is always on record.
