2 Purpose of Partnership

This partnership allows the Partner to list, promote, and manage properties on Estate Web's platform for the purpose of connecting with buyers, renters, and property seekers across Nigeria.

The Partner acknowledges that Estate Web is a listing and technology platform only — not a real estate broker, agent, or legal adviser — and that the Partner is solely responsible for the accuracy, legality, and conduct of their listings and transactions.

3 Scope of Services

The following services are available to Partners depending on their tier. Nothing beyond what is defined here is implied or granted.

Property Listings

Post and manage property listings (for sale, lease, or rent) within your tier limits. Standard vs. Featured/Sponsored placement available on Premium.

Lead Generation

Receive property enquiries via email, WhatsApp, or direct call. Premium Partners may receive CRM-synced leads via our API.

Company Profile & Branding

Premium Partners receive a branded Company Profile page including agent bios, office locations, and logo display.

Analytics & Reporting

Access to dashboard data covering listing views, clicks, and lead conversion rates. Basic Partners receive monthly PDF summaries; Premium receive real-time dashboards.

CRM & API Integration

Premium Partners may access our API to push leads directly into their internal CRM software. Subject to separate API terms.

Advertising & Promotion

Estate Web may feature Partner listings in marketing campaigns, social media, and promotional content. See Section 18 for full details.

If a service is not listed in this section or your tier plan, the Partner does not have permission to use it. Unauthorised use constitutes a material breach of this agreement.

4 Partner Responsibilities

By entering this partnership, the Partner must:

  • Provide accurate, complete, and up-to-date property information at all times
  • Only list properties they own or are legally authorised to represent
  • Update or remove listings within 24–48 hours of any status change (sold, leased, price change)
  • Respond to buyer/tenant enquiries professionally and within the timeframe required by their tier
  • Comply with all applicable Nigerian laws including the Land Use Act, NDPR 2019, NDPA 2023, and Consumer Protection laws
  • Maintain a valid means of identification and business registration documents on file with Estate Web
  • Ensure all listing images, descriptions, and pricing are genuine and not copied without authorisation
  • Cooperate with Estate Web in any investigation into suspected fraud or misconduct

The Partner is solely responsible for their listings, content, and all transactions with users. Estate Web is not a party to any property transaction and assumes no liability for Partner conduct.

5 Estate Web Responsibilities

Estate Web agrees to:

  • Provide platform access in accordance with the Partner's selected tier
  • Maintain reasonable uptime (target 99.9% — see Section 14 for SLA details)
  • Offer support as per the Partner's plan
  • Notify Partners of material changes to these terms with reasonable notice
  • Protect Partner and user data in accordance with our Privacy Policy and NDPR obligations

Estate Web does not guarantee sales, leads, listing visibility, or the success of any property transaction. Platform performance depends on many factors outside our direct control.

6.4 Non-Payment & Suspension

Failure to pay within 7 days of the due date will result in automatic account suspension. Access will be restored upon full payment of outstanding amounts. Continued non-payment beyond 30 days may result in permanent termination and removal of all listings.

Late payments may attract a fee of up to 5% of the outstanding balance per month at Estate Web's discretion. All amounts are in Nigerian Naira (₦) unless otherwise stated.

"Stale" listings — properties that have been sold, rented, or are no longer available — must be removed immediately and in any event within 48 hours of the status change. Repeated stale listings may result in account suspension without refund.

9 Data Protection & Privacy

This section is governed by and should be read alongside Estate Web's full Privacy Policy. Both parties agree to comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) 2019 and the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023.

The Partner agrees to:

  • Handle all user data — including buyer/renter enquiries and contact details — with strict confidentiality

  • Report any suspected data breach involving Estate Web user data within 24 hours

Misuse of user data — including unsolicited marketing, data resale, or scraping — will result in immediate termination and may be reported to the Nigeria Data Protection Bureau (NDPB).

11. Fraud & Misconduct — Zero Tolerance

Estate Web operates a zero-tolerance policy on fraud, deception, and misconduct. The following actions will result in immediate account termination without notice or refund:

  • Posting fake, fabricated, or fraudulent property listings
  • Misrepresenting ownership or authorisation to list a property
  • "Bait and switch" tactics — advertising properties at false prices to capture leads
  • Collecting payments, deposits, or "inspection fees" outside the scope of a legitimate transaction
  • Listing properties with disputed ownership, active litigation, or 'Omo Onile' conflicts without disclosure
  • Harassing, threatening, or abusing users or Estate Web staff
  • Creating multiple accounts to circumvent a suspension
  • Using the platform to facilitate money laundering or other illegal activities
Estate Web reserves the right to remove listings, suspend accounts, and permanently ban users without prior warning where fraud or misconduct is suspected. We may also report suspected fraud to the EFCC, Nigerian Police, or other relevant authorities.

The Partner agrees not to circumvent the Estate Web platform to gain unfair advantage or to avoid platform fees. Specifically, the Partner must not:

This obligation does not prevent the Partner from conducting normal business activities outside the platform with clients who were not sourced through Estate Web.
