Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a fee to become a partner?

No. Partnership on Estate Web is completely free during our early launch phase.

Is Estate Web handling transactions?

No. Estate Web does not handle payments or property transactions. We focus on listings, visibility, lead management, and analytics.

How quickly can I see results from the partnership?

Most partners report increased visibility within the first week. Long-term benefits like brand positioning build over 3–6 months.

Can I leave the partnership at any time?

Yes. Partners are free to stop using the platform at any time with no penalties.

How long does the partnership last?

There's no fixed duration. Partnerships continue as long as both parties see value and remain active.

Is Estate Web available nationwide?

Estate Web is launching with a focus on Nigeria specifically, and will expand gradually to other regions.

Will there be paid plans in the future?

Yes. As the platform grows, premium features may be introduced. Early partners will be notified first and may receive special benefits.
