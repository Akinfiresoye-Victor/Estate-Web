Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

{% with faqs='' %}

Is there a fee to become a partner?

No — partnership is completely free during our early launch phase. In the future, premium tiers may be introduced, but you will always be notified well in advance and early partners will receive priority consideration for discounted rates.

Is Estate Web handling property transactions?

No. Estate Web is a listing platform — we do not handle payments, escrow, or transactions. We focus on visibility, lead capture, and analytics. All deals happen directly between you and your clients.

How quickly can I see results?

Most partners begin getting enquiries within the first week. Meaningful lead flow typically builds over 3–6 months. As an early partner, you benefit from being established before the platform reaches full scale.

Can I leave the partnership at any time?

Yes, absolutely. On a free plan there are no penalties. Your listings will be removed within 5 business days of your departure.

How long does the partnership last?

There is no fixed end date. Partnerships continue as long as both parties are active and terms are met. We are building long-term relationships, not short-term arrangements.

Is Estate Web available nationwide?

Yes. Estate Web covers all Nigerian states. Our initial concentration is Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt, but we are actively growing in all major markets. Outside major cities you have less early-partner competition.

Will there be paid plans in the future?

Yes. As the platform grows, premium features will be offered on paid plans. Early partners receive special discounted rates. You will never be switched to a paid plan without clear notice.

How does lead delivery work?

When a user enquires about your listing, their contact details are delivered directly to you via email. You then contact them directly — Estate Web is not involved in the conversation.

What happens if I post a fraudulent listing?

Fake, misleading, or stale listings are removed immediately. Repeated violations result in account suspension. All partners agree to remove sold or rented properties within 48 hours.

How long does the application review take?

Our team aims to review all applications within 3–5 business days. Once approved you will receive an email with onboarding details.
