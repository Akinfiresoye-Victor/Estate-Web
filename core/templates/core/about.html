A clearer way to find property in Nigeria.

Estate Web is being built for people looking to buy or rent a home, and for the agents, companies, and landlords responsible for listing one.

Where things standListing professionals are onboarding. Example properties on the home page are not currently available to view.

It began with a question of purpose.

Estate Web began with prayer and a search for a project that could serve a real need. The next day, a conversation brought Nigeria’s housing challenges into focus.

Property information was scattered, and people often had little clarity about who stood behind a listing. What first seemed like a difficult market problem became a reason to build something more useful.

People looking for a home should be able to understand what is being offered and who to contact about it.

That belief is still the starting point: make the search easier to follow, and keep the connection between a property and the person listing it clear.

A marketplace taking shape.

Estate Web is preparing to bring property seekers and listing professionals together. Here is what is happening now, and what people can expect when listings open.

Right now

Real-estate agents and companies are onboarding ahead of launch. The sample properties shown on the home page are illustrations, not live listings.

When listings go live

People will be able to browse without creating an account, then contact the agent, company, or landlord responsible for a property.

Built with a practical concern for the people involved.

Akinfiresoye Victor

Founder & Lead Engineer

Victor started Estate Web to address problems he saw in the market: fragmented tools, unclear transaction fees, and limited transparency between landlords and tenants.

His focus is to build a reliable place for property information and the people responsible for it. The work starts with a clear foundation and grows alongside the Nigerian property market it serves.
